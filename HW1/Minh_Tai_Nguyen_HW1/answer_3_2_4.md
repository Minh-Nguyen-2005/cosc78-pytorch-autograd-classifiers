With mean subtraction and normalization, my Iris model reached 98.33% training accuracy; retraining the same architecture and schedule with both preprocessing options disabled reached 94.17%. The accuracy drops because the four Iris features have different means and scales, so the raw inputs make optimization less well conditioned and can push tanH units toward saturated regions. Centering and scaling the features gives SGD a better balanced parameter landscape, which improves convergence for the same learning rate and training schedule.
